Help: the microphone does not start
  • Open this page from its https:// link (not from Files / Downloads). Phones only give the microphone to https pages.
  • Android (Chrome): tap the icon left of the address → Permissions → Microphone → Allow. Or Settings → Apps → Chrome → Permissions → Microphone.
  • iPhone (Safari): tap aA in the address bar → Website Settings → Microphone → Allow. Also Settings → Safari → Microphone → Ask/Allow.
  • Close other apps that use the microphone (calls, voice recorder).
  • Keep this page open and the screen on while receiving.
